Silly Cam

Tap the button to turn on the camera, then pick a silly filter.

Everything happens on this device. No account, no uploads. A picture is only kept if you choose to save it.

For grown-ups

This page uses the camera only to draw filters on the screen. Video never leaves the device, and the page makes no requests to any other website. The face-tracking model is a file that downloads with the page and runs locally.

On an iPhone or iPad, open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full-screen like an app. Open it from the icon once while online, and after that it keeps working without internet.

Face filters need iOS 16.4 or newer. On older devices the picture filters still work.

"Save picture" opens the normal iOS share sheet, where Save Image puts the picture in Photos. Nothing is saved unless you do that.

If the camera button does nothing, check Settings > Apps > Safari > Camera, and allow it for this site.

Press and hold the picture to save it.
